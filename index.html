    How to read the map

    Zoom with + and −, Ctrl + scroll or a trackpad pinch, then drag to move around. Double-click zooms in; Fit shows the whole map again.

    Rings show depth, from Beginner (inner) to Advanced (outer). Sectors group notions by category; select a sector name to filter. Dashed lines link related notions. Planets with an orbit carry moons: the models or products of that family.

          How to use it

          Select a star or a catalog tile to open its card. Use the arrow keys ← → to move through notions in map order, and press / to search.

          Mastery index weights each notion: Exploring 1, Practicing 2, Mastered 3, out of a maximum of 156.

          About the data

          Definitions and examples were written for this guide. Sources link to official documentation or the original papers.

          Star age is the year a notion emerged. Heat and black holes are an editorial view as of October 2026: worth revisiting every few months.

          Your statuses and field notes are private: no one else can see them, the page owner included. The author's statuses and the notes marked Shared are visible to everyone who opens the page; notes marked Private stay with the author.

          If you turn on “Share my levels”, your Practicing and Mastered statuses (never your notes) become visible to the team.

          Deep Field objects are curated by the guide's author, with a weekly scan of research and industry sources.

          Missions are visible to everyone who opens the page. Each person edits only their own; the page owner can remove an entry if needed.

          Export My Journey saves a copy of your statuses, your notes, your Missions and your Library items as a Markdown file on your computer. It contains only what is yours.
